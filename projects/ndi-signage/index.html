The idea

These Godot displays present data from the company’s existing backend. The art team supplies designs and assets; I implement the UI and display logic. Most of the studio’s Godot projects use NDI to support flexible marketing feeds.

Currency exchange rate board

I independently developed the currency-rate board in under a month in 2023. It reads and directly displays backend rates, which are set through an existing internal application, using a Godot UI built from art-team designs.

Casino trend boards

I developed roulette-board variants and contributed to baccarat trend boards alongside other developers, including rolling-number displays and mini trend boards. I implemented the Godot UI and logic from supplied artwork; roulette multipliers, game state and results come from the backend.

Keypads and trend boards communicate through the backend: keypads update game state, results and table settings; trend boards read those values for display.

The baccarat examples above are display-team products. My contributions to this family include roulette variants, rolling-number displays and mini trend boards; the art team supplied the designs and assets.

Multi-feed research: streamerer & denomor

I built streamerer and the denomor prototype to receive and arrange multiple NDI feeds, then output separate background and foreground feeds, including a transparent foreground feed.

In denomor, I implemented local-video fallback when NDI input is unavailable. The app attempts to download videos from the backend at startup.

I completed the denomor research prototype and handed it over to colleagues, who developed it into a production product.

Outcome

The currency-rate board and trend boards launched and remain in use. I no longer maintain the currency board; the team continues to maintain the roulette and trend boards. My display-development contributions covered 2023–2025. Colleagues developed the denomor prototype into a production product.
