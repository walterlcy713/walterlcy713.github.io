The brief

Boxing and Racing were developed for the entrance LED wall at Studio City Macau. Visitors registered at a nearby kiosk to join the queue, then controlled the main gameplay entirely through camera input, without handheld controllers or wearable devices. Interactive gameplay was scheduled from 11:00 to 18:00.

Boxing was already in proof-of-concept development by late June 2024. On-site setup, fine-tuning and bug fixes began on 22 September, and Boxing launched on 26 September. Racing development followed Boxing’s launch; Racing launched on 1 November 2024.

On-site setup & testing

For Boxing, I worked on site from 22 to 27 September with another developer and a colleague responsible for hardware deployment. My work covered software setup, on-site testing, immediate bug fixes and fine-tuning through the 26 September launch.

For Racing, much of the hardware and software setup was already in place from Boxing. I handled software setup, testing and fixes as the only on-site developer from our team, with remote support from another developer at the company.

The games

Built in Unreal Engine with ZED stereo cameras for player tracking. I implemented most of the game logic and gameplay data structures, and connected ZED tracking data to gameplay. Camera tracking and ZED input were developed jointly with another Unreal developer. Another developer and I also researched, configured and tested DLSS; gameplay was noticeably smoother with it enabled than without it.

The development team had five people, including me: three Unreal developers, one Unity kiosk developer and one backend developer. I assigned supporting work to colleagues, who implemented WebSocket communication with the backend, secondary gameplay on two pillars linked to the main game, and the player-registration and queueing kiosk.

My hands-on work included game and level design, NPC action design and UI/UX implementation. I modified the main gameplay to integrate colleagues’ data and features, tested the complete game, and coordinated the connections between components. I also reviewed and validated colleagues’ UI and feature implementations.

Another Unreal developer and I shared coordination with the art team, including work planning, interface coordination and technical support for Unreal scenes and Blender model and animation exports.

I tested the registration kiosk, the first step in the player journey, and reported bugs to a remote colleague who implemented the fixes. For Boxing, visitors selected their gender and a preset name, then had their photo taken. Racing added a car-selection step. My technical support spanned development, on-site setup, and the period before and after launch.

OAK camera integration

I built the OAK camera integration, from camera testing and DepthAI SDK integration to reading and adjusting camera data in Python and bringing it into Unity. I also arranged camera video in Unity and output it through the NDI SDK.

Boxing and Racing use ZED for the main-screen gameplay and six OAK cameras on each of the two pillars. The Unity app calculates a camera-derived value called depth%, used for spectator-assisted gameplay.

As spectators move closer to the cameras, depth% increases. In Boxing, this speeds up special-attack charging and increases attack power; in Racing, it increases the car’s speed. Both games have timed leaderboards, and this mechanic encourages spectators to help the active player finish faster.

I configured camera IPs and flashed bootloaders using official DepthAI tools, with help from the deployment colleague who determined the static-IP assignments. I also implemented logic to stop depth calculations when no game was running.

Movie-trailer effects

I built the OAK camera-to-NDI pipeline for the movie-trailer installation. Artists used the camera feeds in TouchDesigner to create the visual effects.

Outcome

Boxing and the movie-trailer effects launched at Studio City’s front entrance on 26 September 2024. Racing followed on 1 November 2024. The games let spectators assist the active player through camera-driven gameplay boosts.
